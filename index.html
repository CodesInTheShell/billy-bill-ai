Billy Bill AI

A community-driven way to shape legislative proposals.

Billy Bill AI helps people turn shared concerns into structured legislative proposals and policy drafts. AI assists with organization and language while the community reviews assumptions, suggests changes, and improves each proposal before it is ever considered formal legislation.

Proof of concept only: this is a social testing website/app for the idea that AI and the community can work together to develop better public policy. Published drafts are community proposals, not introduced bills or enacted law.

Start with a policy need

Community members identify a public problem, the people affected, and the outcome a proposal should pursue.

AI-powered drafting

AI helps organize public ideas into a clear rationale, policy approach, and set of draft provisions.

Community-sourced ideas

People challenge assumptions, suggest edits, and improve proposals so they better reflect public priorities.

About Billy Bill AI

Billy Bill AI is a proof-of-concept civic platform where people collaboratively develop policy ideas and review AI-assisted legislative proposals in one place.

The goal is to make policy development easier to understand, discuss, and improve. Every item is presented as a community-authored proposal or policy draft—not as an officially filed bill.
